# Reserved — Certification Body platform (OUT OF SCOPE for TPS Platform V2)

Per the **TPS Platform V2 Project Constitution**, the certification-body business is **not** part of
this repository/platform. **TPS Xperts Global Certifications Pvt. Ltd.** is a **separate legal entity**
and will have its **own independent platform** in future.

The following are **excluded** from the TPS Platform (this repo):
- Certification Body Operations
- ISO/IEC 17021 Internal QMS
- NABCB Internal Management
- Auditor Management
- Certification Workflow

The two design docs in this folder were produced during the enterprise-architecture design phase and
are **retained here only as a head-start for the future, separate Certification platform.** They are
**not** implemented in this platform and are **not** part of the frozen v2.0 module portfolio.

- `certification.md` — Certification Body (ISO 17021) design
- `management-system.md` — Internal QMS / NABCB self-management design

**Do not implement these here.** The in-scope modules for this platform are listed in
`../01_MODULE_PORTFOLIO.md` and `../MASTER_PLATFORM_BLUEPRINT.md`.
